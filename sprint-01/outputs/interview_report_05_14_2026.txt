# Customer Interview Analysis Report

**Date:** 2026-05-14
**File:** customer_interviews.csv

---

## Summary

Out of 10 contacts, 4 interviews are completed, 4 are scheduled, and 2 have not been contacted. Of the 8 respondents who provided pain points, 6 are willing to pay, 2 are maybe, and 0 said no.

---

## Top Themes Identified

1. **Time-consuming, manual report generation** — Multiple respondents (John Martinez, Sarah Chen, Chris Williams) highlighted that creating inspection reports is a tedious, time-intensive manual process that pulls them away from higher-value fieldwork.

2. **Inconsistent report quality and lack of standardization** — Amanda Foster and Sarah Chen pointed to the problem of reports varying in quality across inspectors and being created from scratch each time, suggesting a need for templates or automated formatting.

3. **Insufficient documentation for client and stakeholder needs** — Lisa Rodriguez and Jennifer Lee noted that customers, insurers, and other stakeholders demand detailed, professional documentation that their current workflows cannot easily produce.

4. **Limited tracking and visual comparison capabilities** — Mike Thompson and Emily White expressed frustration with the inability to track panel performance over time or provide before/after comparisons, indicating a gap in longitudinal data management and visualization.

---

## Recommendation

**What to do next:** Prioritize building a standardized, automated report generation tool with templates, before/after comparison features, and performance tracking — then schedule the remaining 2 uncontacted leads and validate this solution direction with the 4 upcoming scheduled interviews.
